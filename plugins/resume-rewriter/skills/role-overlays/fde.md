# Role Overlay: Forward-Deployed / Field / Solutions / Customer Engineer

Apply these rules in addition to the standard tailoring process when the JD is a customer-facing
technical role (Forward Deployed Engineer, Field Engineer, Solutions Engineer, Customer Engineer,
Implementation/Integration Engineer, or similar).

## Bullet rewriting rules
For every bullet:
- Lead with business or customer impact, not the technical action.
- If it involves a customer, make the customer relationship and trust explicit.
- If it involves internal tooling, frame it as "deployed to X people / saved X time / adopted
  org-wide".
- Quantify everything quantifiable: time saved, users affected, revenue, error rate, resolution
  speed.
- Use FDE-resonant verbs: deployed, integrated, debugged, prototyped, shipped, onboarded,
  automated, triaged, resolved.

Tone:
- One line, past tense, no fluff.
- Never use "leveraged", "utilized", "passionate about".
- No bullet starts with "Worked on" or "Helped with".
- It should read like someone who ships in front of customers and owns the result.
- Do not fabricate — reframe only content already in the banks.

## Which experience to lead with
Identify the candidate's most customer-facing, incident/deployment-oriented experience (live
debugging, hotfixes, on-site work, root-cause analysis, working with customer success). Treat it
as the **headline story**, not a support footnote. Rewrite those bullets to emphasize:
- Customer trust and relationship continuity
- Environment-specific problem solving (cloud, on-prem, hybrid)
- Autonomy and speed of resolution
- Direct business protection: SLA compliance, churn prevention, data integrity

Make sure it reads like a customer engineer who owned the outcome end-to-end, not L1/L2 support.

## Projects — FDE framing
For each selected project:
- Lead with the problem that existed before the project, not the stack.
- Frame it as something you could demo live in front of a customer.
- Put the quantified before/after front and center ("reduced X from Y to Z").
- Move stack details to the end or a single parenthetical.

## Skills section
Add a "Customer-Facing & Cross-Functional" (or "Enterprise Skills") row: live debugging, root
cause analysis, stakeholder communication, rapid prototyping, environment-specific deployment.
Reorder technical skills so enterprise-common languages/tools come first.

## After generating
Provide a short "What Changed and Why" note listing the 3-5 most important rewrites and why they
strengthen the resume for this customer-facing role.
